## Upstream

Atomwright is an independent fork of
[Gentle AI](https://github.com/Gentleman-Programming/gentle-ai); see
[`NOTICE.md`](NOTICE.md). The inherited implementation has since been removed
(see [ADR-0002](docs/adr/0002-atomwright-identity-and-greenfield-baseline.md)),
but the project started from their work.

Gentle AI's own contributors are credited in
[that project's CONTRIBUTORS.md](https://github.com/Gentleman-Programming/gentle-ai/blob/main/CONTRIBUTORS.md).
They are not listed here, because listing them would imply they contributed to
Atomwright.
